Sorry, and Goodnight

a little letter from a very sorry bunny

scroll slowly, I have something to say ↓
first, the big one

I'm sorry that I hurt your heart,
I never meant to tear us apart.
My ears are floppy, my paws are shy,
and there's a tiny tear in my eye.

what I got wrong

I'm sorry for the words I said,
they're still bouncing in my head.
I should have listened, soft and slow,
instead I let the grumpy show.

a small bouquet

I picked a flower for every "oops,"
and tied them up in tiny loops.
They're wobbly, I admit it's true,
but every petal says I love you.

my promise

I promise I will try my best,
to talk it out, then hug, then rest.
And if I slip, please tell me so,
I'm still learning how to grow.

now, goodnight

The moon is out, the stars are too,
they're all whispering "night" to you.
Close your eyes and snuggle in,
let your sweet dreams now begin.

tuck-in time

You don't have to forgive me tonight.
Just rest. I'll be here in the morning.
